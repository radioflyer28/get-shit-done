## Overview

GSD Skill Lifecycle Tooling v1.0 adds create, audit, and tune capabilities for building and improving GSD skills. Three integrated tools (`/gsd-build-skill`, `/gsd-audit-skill`, `/gsd-tune-skill`) share a common infrastructure of SMART criteria and structural conventions, enabling every GSD skill to meet a consistent, verifiable quality bar.

**Core Achievement:** Shipped a complete skill lifecycle with shared quality framework, comprehensive auditing, guided creation, and human-in-the-loop improvement.

### Key Decisions

1. **SMART as quality framework** — 5 dimensions map well to prompt quality evaluation
2. **Auditor as shared backbone** — Avoids duplicate logic; scaffolder validates via auditor, tuner diagnoses via auditor
3. **Semantic prioritization for tuner** — Symptom-driven diagnosis improves finding relevance over raw audit results
4. **Human-in-the-loop with 3 iterations** — Balances safety (approval gate) with efficiency (limited refinements)
5. **Batch mode for scale** — Enables milestone-scale quality improvement with atomic commits
6. **Transcript extraction** — Friction signals from real sessions improve symptom understanding and diff prioritization
